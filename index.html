Backup & sharing procedure

Use these steps for safe daily operation.

  1. Before opening, confirm menu prices and restaurant details.
  2. Create each guest bill and check quantities, discounts and totals.
  3. Save the bill. Use Print / Save PDF to make a PDF copy.
  4. For WhatsApp, open the invoice's WhatsApp share action, confirm the recipient and send the prepared message. Attach the saved PDF manually if you want the full invoice document.
  5. For email, open the email action, confirm the guest's address, attach the saved PDF in your email app and press Send.
  6. At closing, open Backup data and save the JSON backup somewhere safe, such as a separate USB drive or secure folder.
This local version does not send messages silently or attach files automatically. It opens WhatsApp or your email app for you to review and send.
